There is a doctrinal trap here, too. Layer three should not become Layer two. The Suricata-grade inspection that lives here is slower and richer than what runs at the edge. It is allowed to take more time per flow. But it must not lag behind the attacker's operational tempo. An I-D-S that takes minutes to surface a hit has missed the window for lateral movement. The Hauptkampffeld is slow only in the sense of "slower than the edge". It is fast compared to the attacker's planning cycle, and lateral movement — the scan, the credential reuse, the pivot — is the part of the kill chain where the attacker is slowest and most exposed. That is the window the deep zone exists to close.
